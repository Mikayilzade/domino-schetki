## What was run

- 386 deterministic deals generated from seed window 3446032–3446417; 200 late positions selected, one per eligible seed. The exact selected set is reproducible from the window and the runner’s deterministic selection rule.
- Generator policies alternate by seed parity: min-hand-pips / fast-doubles, with action-orbit symmetric tie-break.
- Selected positions have at least two legal actions, both opponents at 3 stones or fewer, and a prior no-stock pass by a hidden seat.
- 362 pass observations were retained. The pass-time hand is reconstructed as the current hand plus every tile that the same seat played after the pass. The sample has no later stock acquisition.
- Exact labeled hidden-hand allocations were enumerated. No opponent-choice likelihood was applied; worlds are weighted uniformly conditional on the public pass constraints.
- No candidate strategy comparisons were run in this checkpoint.

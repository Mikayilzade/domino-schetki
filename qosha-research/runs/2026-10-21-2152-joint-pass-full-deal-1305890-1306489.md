## Finding, confidence, and limitations

**Finding:** conditioning on both opponents' pass histories improves next-pass probability calibration, but **does not establish an improvement in finish-first probability** in this fresh matched full-round experiment. This is consistent with the earlier disagreement between calibration and selected-position regret; do not promote a universal pass-based strategy rule.

Confidence: high for deterministic execution/termination and the observed descriptive metrics, limited for a population-level strategic effect (paired confidence intervals cross zero; only 600 independent deals and two deterministic bot profiles).

Assumptions: provisional four-branch opening semantics; all unseen tiles uniformly partitioned into the two opponents' exact hand sizes after accounting for known tiles and no-stock pass exclusions; no likelihood weighting for earlier policy decisions; fixed deterministic bot opponents; no block winner; no human-play generalization.

**Finding (provisional):** when the discarded pip sums are equal, opening a double immediately was substantially worse than the matched ordinary move against both simulated opponent profiles. Next-opponent passes also fell after the double, consistent with opening mobility to the opponent, but not proof of causality.

**Critical limits:** this is not a universal real-game rule. Lexicographic first-pair selection, first-eligible trajectories, and branch-state semantics can bias the effect; equal pips do not equalize board changes. Hidden opponent tiles were sampled uniformly conditional on recorded no-stock passes, without policy-likelihood conditioning. Block winner is not assigned; finish-first is not match win. Confidence intervals are exploratory and not multiple-comparison adjusted. The 51,200 continuations are not independent deals.
